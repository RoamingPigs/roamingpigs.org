# RoamingPigs Systems & AI Research Lab

Official web portal and research commons for **RoamingPigs Lab** (`https://roamingpigs.org`).

## Research Pillars

1. **Tactical Radio Channel Simulator (TRCS):** Narrowband acoustic telephony degradation and hallucination benchmarks for speech foundation models.
2. **Verifier Error Covariance & Optimal Stopping (VEC-SCR):** Mathematical formulation of sequential stopping under positive error dependence in budgeted test-time compute.
3. **Sovereign APU Bandwidth & Energy Physics:** Hardware telemetry, 256.0 GB/s memory bus saturation, and Joules-per-token profiling on unified memory APUs.
