Energy and speed per token on one mini PC: Vulkan against ROCm

Four local language models at four context lengths, three repeats each, on two software backends, measured at the wall plug and at the chip. I wrote the plan and my expectations down first and pushed them before the run.

What this does not show #

  • Anything about other hardware, other chips, discrete GPUs, other ROCm versions or packaging, or other builds of llama.cpp.
  • The accuracy of the wall watts. The plug is uncalibrated and reads whole watts; its own energy counter and the sum of its readings differ by {{ st.stats.counter_gap }}%, but both come from the same plug.
  • Batched or concurrent serving. Everything is one stream at a time.
  • Quality of the output. The prompts are licence texts and the replies are not scored.
  • That anyone outside this project has repeated it.

Three earlier starts of this run were stopped after a few cells each because outside load reached the machine (requests from an unknown caller on the network, backup pushes from two other computers, a backup job on the machine itself). The last start had no failed cell and no contaminated cell. The whole account is in the repository's DEVIATIONS.md.
